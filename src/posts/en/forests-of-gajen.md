<p>The new ICRPG Core expansion is on <a href="https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/458356/ICRPG-Forests-of-Gajen">drivethrurpg</a>!</p>
<p>Join adventures on the path through the Dark Forests of Gajen. Meet deadly creatures of the night, and test yourself against the poisoned bog of Imlebin. Find the truth behind the mysterious Frozen Blade, a weapon from the forgotten past.</p>
<p>This game module includes:</p>
<ul>
<li>120 ICRPG cards with:
<ul>
<li>new heroes and creatures</li>
<li>tavern tiles</li>
<li>bogs and marshes</li>
<li>caves, caverns, and black lake</li>
<li>dark woods, villages, and goblin camps</li>
<li>loot elements</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>34 pages of storyline, ideas, alchemy, and a cool map</li>
<li>13 pages of monsters and heroes minis</li>
<li>30 VTT tokens</li>
</ul>
<p>To play, it is required to use ICRPG Core v2 rules. Please find more <a href="https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/212262/INDEX-CARD-RPG-Core-2E">here</a>.</p>
