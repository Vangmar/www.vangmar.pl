<p>Today something short, little tip tested regarding adding weight to the foam elements. You know the problem, with small effort you can&nbsp;blow them away from the table, especially those little bits you’ve created. Better to add weight to them. How?</p>
<p><span id="more-180"></span></p>
<p>I tried something very simple with pennies. You can find other tutorials all around the net, Runhammer does it also. Very simple. You need just to cut to hole in bottom part of your sculpt.</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144742.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-185 size-large" src="/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144742-e1507626077115-1024x1024.jpg" alt="" width="525" height="525" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144742-e1507626077115-1024x1024.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144742-e1507626077115-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144742-e1507626077115-300x300.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144742-e1507626077115-768x768.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144742-e1507626077115-100x100.jpg 100w" sizes="(max-width: 525px) 100vw, 525px"></a></p>
<p>Then you push penny inside of the hole with hot glue. Use more glue you think you will need. Cleanup is easier, than starting again, because it become detached.</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144815.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-182 size-large" src="/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144815-e1507626108549-1024x1024.jpg" alt="" width="525" height="525" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144815-e1507626108549-1024x1024.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144815-e1507626108549-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144815-e1507626108549-300x300.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144815-e1507626108549-768x768.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144815-e1507626108549-100x100.jpg 100w" sizes="(max-width: 525px) 100vw, 525px"></a></p>
<p>And you got it! Quick, simple and easy – really works!</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144830-e1507625948313.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-183 size-large" src="/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144830-e1507625948313-1024x1024.jpg" alt="" width="525" height="525" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144830-e1507625948313-1024x1024.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144830-e1507625948313-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144830-e1507625948313-300x300.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144830-e1507625948313-768x768.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/10/20170301_144830-e1507625948313-100x100.jpg 100w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 525px) 100vw, 525px"></a></p>
<p>Now you can paint it and… to the table!</p>
