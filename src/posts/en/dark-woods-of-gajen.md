<p>Trick or threat?</p>
<p>I definitely prefer trick. And in next couple of days I like to share with you my fantasy setting called The Dark Woods of Gajen. Welcome to the evil forest, where anything you see may turn into your worst enemy.</p>

<p>I like to start with crafting tutorials, so you will be able to find out how to make your own campfire and evil wood. I also got some marshes bits and the central stone of ancient force hidden inside of it.</p>
<p>After ages broken force circle caused this ancient dream creature to wake up and bring Raktahalm nightmares again to this old forests.</p>
<p>Yes adventuress, your enemy is an ancient stone hidden deep in to cursed woods. Beware! And be brave! Fight is coming!</p>
