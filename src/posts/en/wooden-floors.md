<p>It is time for wooden floor for our D&amp;D bridges and taverns!</p>
<p>And we make it two sided.</p>
<p><span id="more-47"></span></p>
<h3>Preparation</h3>
<p>First you will need to take sheet of polystyrene foam. I’ve have here one like ~20mm thick. For each floor part we will need two same size pieces. Here I got six 8×2 inc. parts.</p>
<p>On three of them I added flagstone cuts with my sharp knife. You can find also some breaks to show how old those road parts are. With sandpaper I also added another bit of destruction (it will look nice at the end).</p>
<p>Next step is to paint with base black paint them from all sides. You should have something like below.</p>
<p><a href="http://serwer19432.lh.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728460.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-48 size-large" src="http://vangmar.5v.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728460-1024x1024.jpg" alt="" width="525" height="525" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728460-1024x1024.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728460-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728460-300x300.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728460-768x768.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728460-100x100.jpg 100w" sizes="(max-width: 525px) 100vw, 525px"></a></p>
<p>Wait until it becomes dry. Then paint one side with dark grey and light grey layers. I’ve made this with dry brush. You will something like this…</p>
<p><a href="http://serwer19432.lh.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234412-1.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-100 size-large" src="http://vangmar.5v.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234412-1-1024x1024.jpg" alt="" width="525" height="525" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234412-1-1024x1024.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234412-1-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234412-1-300x300.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234412-1-768x768.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234412-1-100x100.jpg 100w" sizes="(max-width: 525px) 100vw, 525px"></a></p>
<h3>Time to start hard work!</h3>
<p>You have to cut second part of styro. into planks. &nbsp;You can first cut into 1×1 inc. part. Then each of those parts need to be cut into 3 separate planks.&nbsp;This will allow you to create a mosaic. This shape will allow your players (and you as DM!) easily find hero position during battle.</p>
<p><a href="http://serwer19432.lh.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728530.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-49 size-large" style="font-size: 1rem;" src="http://vangmar.5v.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728530-1024x1024.jpg" alt="" width="525" height="525" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728530-1024x1024.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728530-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728530-300x300.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728530-768x768.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_1728530-100x100.jpg 100w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 525px) 100vw, 525px"></a></p>
<p>Next step will be to paint them with Burned Umber color (no black base paint this time!) Light color of foam will be our base here.</p>
<p><a href="http://serwer19432.lh.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_172909.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-50 size-large" style="font-size: 1rem;" src="http://vangmar.5v.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_172909-1024x1024.jpg" alt="" width="525" height="525" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_172909-1024x1024.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_172909-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_172909-300x300.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_172909-768x768.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170204_172909-100x100.jpg 100w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 525px) 100vw, 525px"></a><span style="font-size: 1rem;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<h3>Binding all of it together</h3>
<p>You should finish with a number of these little nice planks and 3 parts for stone floor. Now you have to bind them with hot glue.</p>
<p><a href="http://serwer19432.lh.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234427-1.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-101 size-large" src="http://vangmar.5v.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234427-1-1024x1024.jpg" alt="" width="525" height="525" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234427-1-1024x1024.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234427-1-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234427-1-300x300.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234427-1-768x768.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_234427-1-100x100.jpg 100w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 525px) 100vw, 525px"></a><a href="http://serwer19432.lh.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_235520.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-53 size-large" style="font-size: 1rem;" src="http://vangmar.5v.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_235520-1024x1024.jpg" alt="" width="525" height="525" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_235520-1024x1024.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_235520-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_235520-300x300.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_235520-768x768.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170206_235520-100x100.jpg 100w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 525px) 100vw, 525px"></a></p>
<p>You take 3 planks and glue them into dark side of floor part. Keep eye on the sizes! 3 planks should fill whole 1×1” and no more. Also keep in mind to create mosaic from them. So 3 planks on one inc. goes&nbsp;horizontally and 3 on next goes&nbsp;vertically.</p>
<p><a href="http://serwer19432.lh.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170207_000108.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-55 size-large" style="font-size: 1rem;" src="http://vangmar.5v.pl/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170207_000108-1024x1024.jpg" alt="" width="525" height="525" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170207_000108-1024x1024.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170207_000108-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170207_000108-300x300.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170207_000108-768x768.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2017/09/20170207_000108-100x100.jpg 100w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 525px) 100vw, 525px"></a></p>
